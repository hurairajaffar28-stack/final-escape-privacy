1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how FINAL ESCAPE ("the Game", "the Application", "we", "us", or "our"), developed by Huraira Jaffar, handles data when you install and play our game on Android devices.

Core Privacy Commitment: FINAL ESCAPE is built as an offline-first 2D action runner. We do not require account registration, do not maintain first-party remote user databases, and do not track your personal identity or sell your data.

The Game integrates certified developer libraries provided by Google LLC to deliver optional, user-initiated rewarded advertisements and facilitate user privacy consent management. This policy outlines how those services operate.

2. Information We (The Developer) Do NOT Collect

The Application does not collect, harvest, store, or transmit any personal identifying information on first-party servers:

  • No Account Information: No usernames, email addresses, phone numbers, passwords, or social network credentials.
  • No Proprietary Analytics: No custom tracking servers, telemetry trackers, or profiling systems.
  • No Payment Data: The Game contains zero real-money in-app microtransactions or subscription purchases. No financial data is ever collected or processed.
  • No Sensitive Hardware Access: The Game does not request access to device cameras, microphones, address books, photos, storage media, or precise GPS location.

3. Local Storage on Your Device

To preserve gameplay continuity between play sessions, non-personal game progress is saved strictly on your local device using standard Android SharedPreferences:

  • Highest run score achieved and maximum distance traveled
  • Banked in-game rewards (Coins and Energy Crystals earned through gameplay)
  • Audio settings (Music and Sound Effects volume sliders)
  • Control mode preference (Auto-Run or Manual Run)
  • Tutorial intro state

This data is isolated within the Android application sandbox on your device and is never transmitted to any external server. You can delete this data at any time by clearing application storage in Android system settings or by uninstalling the Game.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising SDKs

To keep FINAL ESCAPE free for players without paywalls, the Game incorporates certified third-party developer libraries from Google LLC.

4.1 Google Mobile Ads (AdMob SDK v23.6.0)

The Game utilizes optional, player-initiated Rewarded Advertisements:

  • Rewarded Revive: Watch an optional video ad to revive your runner once during an active run.
  • Rewarded Double Score: Watch an optional video ad on the Game Over screen to double your completed run score.

FINAL ESCAPE contains zero forced full-screen interstitial ads and zero banner ads during active gameplay.

When you choose to view a rewarded ad, Google AdMob may process certain technical data:

  • Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID), Android Privacy Sandbox Ad Services ID, and IP address (used to infer coarse, city/regional-level location for appropriate regional ad serving).
  • Interaction Metrics: Ad impressions, completion events, click signals, and reward verification callbacks.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Device model, OS version, screen resolution, connection speed, and SDK diagnostic reports.

Google's data practices are detailed in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps.

4.2 Google User Messaging Platform (UMP SDK v3.1.0)

In compliance with regional regulations (including the EU GDPR and UK Data Protection laws), the Game integrates Google's User Messaging Platform. Eligible users within the EEA, UK, and Switzerland are presented with a consent dialogue conforming to the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF v2.2) to manage advertising preferences.

5. Purpose of Data Processing

Data Category Primary Purpose Recipient / Processor
Advertising Identifiers (GAID, Ad Services ID) Serving requested rewarded video ads & fraud prevention Google LLC (AdMob)
Coarse Location (Inferred via IP) Geographic ad routing & regional legal compliance Google LLC (AdMob)
Diagnostic & SDK Error Telemetry Monitoring video playback reliability & stability Google LLC (AdMob)
Privacy Consent Flags Recording GDPR/TCF consent preferences locally Google LLC (UMP) / On-device

6. Data Security & Encryption

All communications initiated by integrated Google SDKs are transmitted using industry-standard transport layer encryption (HTTPS / TLS). We do not operate external intermediate servers or data brokers.

7. Player Controls & Opt-Out Options

You can manage how advertising identifiers are utilized on your Android device at any time:

  • Reset Advertising ID: Navigate to Settings > Google > Ads and tap Reset advertising ID.
  • Delete Advertising ID: In the same menu, tap Delete advertising ID to opt out of personalized ad targeting across all applications.
  • Consent Management: Players in the EEA/UK can update their consent choices through Google UMP dialogues when presented.

8. Children's Privacy

FINAL ESCAPE is a stylized cyberpunk action game intended for a general audience. The Game is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum legal age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. AdMob initialization flags are configured with setTagForUnderAgeOfConsent(false). If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe personal data has been inadvertently collected through third-party services, please contact us for prompt review and resolution.
